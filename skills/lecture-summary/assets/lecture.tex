\title{SUBJECT NAME (CODE)\\[2pt]
       \large Lecture N --- the one-line description of what this lecture did}
\author{Lecturer}
\date{Weekday D Month YYYY}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\noindent Three or four sentences: where the lecture starts, what it builds, and
where it ends up. Write this last. Name the loose end, if it leaves one.
\end{abstract}

\section{Recap}

What was carried over, and why it was needed here.

\section{First section}

Follow the lecture's order, not a tidier one.

\begin{definition}[Name]
As stated in the lecture.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Name]
The statement.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The proof as given. If it was set as an exercise, say so and record the sketch
the lecturer offered, since that is what the exercise expects.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Aside.}
The anecdote, the warning, the common mistake.

\section*{To do}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item Exercises set in the lecture.
  \item Anything deferred to next time.
  \item Anything flagged and not answered.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Not recovered from the recording}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item What the camera lost (blown-out slides, board below frame).
  \item Anything unreadable after cropping and enlarging.
\end{itemize}
